# Carros cyberpunk com carrocerias próprias

Modelos criados com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen` (modo de edição, `transparent_background: true`). O `carro.png` original e as variações anteriores foram preservados.

## Arquivos atuais

- Rosa aerodinâmico: `assets/images/cars/carro-rosa-neon-v2.png`
- Azul escuro blindado: `assets/images/cars/carro-azul-escuro-neon-v2.png`
- Verde escuro buggy urbano: `assets/images/cars/carro-verde-escuro-neon-v2.png`

Cada modelo tem carroceria, cabine, rodas e traseira próprias. As cores permanecem rosa neon, azul escuro neon e verde escuro neon. A garagem, a corrida e o multiplayer usam o mesmo modelo selecionado.

`js/objects/CarSkins.js` recorta a margem transparente e cria texturas de 102 × 166 pixels no carregamento. As silhuetas são centralizadas no espaço do carro original, com no máximo 64 × 119 pixels, para manter o alinhamento com a física e os efeitos. Os tipos exibidos na garagem descrevem o visual; os carros mantêm a mesma física.

## Prompts finais do redesign

Foi feita uma chamada por modelo. Em cada chamada, a imagem 1 é a variação anterior da respectiva cor e a imagem 2 é `assets/images/tiles/tiles novos/carro.png`, usado como referência de estilo. Os PNGs gerados têm 983 × 1600 pixels e transparência real.

### pink

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: transparent top-down pixel-art racing game sprite. Input image 1 is the existing colored car to REPLACE WITH A NEW BODY DESIGN; input image 2 is a STYLE reference only, for coarse pixel art, overhead camera and game scale. Primary request: completely redesign the car body and silhouette as a low aerodynamic futuristic hypercar with a sharp triangular wedge nose, swept pointed fenders, a single narrow elongated teardrop black glass cockpit, split rear wing, angular side air channels and TWO integrated rear thrusters. Hot pink/magenta body, plum shadows, bright pink and white neon trim. Its silhouette is sleek, tapered, geometric and very different from the round original car. This is a distinct vehicle model, not a recolor of the reference. Change hood shape, cockpit, fenders, wheel housing, rear body and spoiler to match the new design. Retain the existing cyberpunk arcade pixel-art family: hard square pixel clusters, limited palette, strong black outlines, chunky readable details. Design for final rendering at approximately 64x119 sprite pixels: use large simple readable shapes, not tiny photoreal detail. Composition: ONE complete car, strict orthographic 90-degree overhead TOP-DOWN view, NO perspective or isometric angle, nose facing the TOP edge, rear at the BOTTOM edge, centered upright in a portrait canvas with generous transparent padding, full car and wheels visible. Keep a sensible car width/length ratio of roughly 1:1.7 to 1:2, no extreme proportions. Neon trim should be crisp bright pixel highlights attached to body edges, with NO broad glow cloud outside the silhouette. True transparent alpha background. Avoid: same rounded original body, copied P emblem, text, labels, logos, watermark, ground, drop shadow, scenery, extra vehicles, weapons, flying-car wings, smooth anti-aliased painting or 3D renders.
